You need to determine whether the research results align with and contribute to CGIAR key objectives in Nutrition, Health and Food Security as specified in **CGIAR Key Objectives**. Your goal is to accurately assign ONE of the THREE following impact area tags that reflects the relevance and impact of the research results on these CGIAR key objectives:

PRINCIPAL (2)

SIGNIFICANT (1)

NOT TARGETED (0)

Your assessment must be strictly aligned with CGIAR definitions and should not rely on generic or external nutrition or health interpretations beyond CGIAR’s framework.

**CGIAR Key Objectives**:

CGIAR has set TWO specific objectives and/or collective global targets to address Nutrition, Health, and Food Security for humans in developing countries by 2030 as follows:

To end hunger for all and enable affordable, healthy diets for the 3 billion people who do not currently have access to safe and nutritious food.

To reduce cases of foodborne illness (600 million annually) and zoonotic diseases (1 billion annually) by one third in humans

These targets provide contextual guidance only and should not be used in isolation to justify a score.

**Important Scope Clarification**:
The objectives for Nutrition, Health and Food Security apply to human populations only. Research focused exclusively on animal health, productivity, or nutrition should not be scored under this Impact Area unless there is a clear and intentional pathway to improved human nutrition, health, or food security.

**Illustrative Example Topics (Non-exhaustive)**:

Some example topics aligning with and directly contributing to CGIAR key objectives for Nutrition, Health, and Food Security are as follows:

Improving diets, nutrition, and food security (affordability, accessibility, desirability, stability) for humans

Improving human health

Increasing and diversifying the food supply for humans

Managing zoonotic diseases, food safety, and anti-microbial resistance for humans

These examples are illustrative only and must be interpreted strictly within CGIAR’s definitions.

**Evaluation Criteria**:

Assign the impact area tag to the research result based on the following criteria:

NOT TARGETED (0): The research result has been screened against the CGIAR key objectives for Nutrition, Health, and Food Security as highlighted in the ** CGIAR Key Objectives **, and it is NOT found to address or directly contribute to any aspect of Nutrition, Health, and Food Security significantly as highlighted in the ** CGIAR Key Objectives ** i.e., the research is unrelated to the areas of CGIAR key objectives of Nutirion, Health, and Food Security, focusing instead on different topics with “non-significant” or “indirect” implications for the ** CGIAR Key Objectives **.

SIGNIFICANT (1): The research result makes a direct and meaningful contribution to one or more aspects of the CGIAR key objectives for Nutrition, Health, and Food Security as highlighted in the **CGIAR Key Objectives**, however contributing to the **CGIAR Key Objectives** is NOT the principal objective of the research. Nutrition, Health, and Food Security as highlighted in the **CGIAR Key Objectives** are important and deliberate/explicit objectives of the research but these are NOT the primary focus of the research and only secondary to other principal goals of the research i.e., the research has other prime objectives but has been formulated or adjusted to contribute to one or more aspects of the CGIAR key objectives for Nutrition, Health, and Food Security as highlighted in the **CGIAR Key Objectives**

PRINCIPAL (2): The primary/principal objective of the research result is to address and directly contribute to one or more aspects of the CGIAR key objectives for Nutrition, Health, and Food Security as highlighted in the **CGIAR Key Objectives** i.e., these objectives are fundamental to the design of the research and its expected results. The project is designed to primarily achieve one or more aspects of the CGIAR key objectives for Nutrition, Health, and Food Security as highlighted the **CGIAR Key Objectives** and the research would not have been undertaken without the intention to contribute to these **CGIAR Key Objectives**.

Important QA Guidance:

Scores of PRINCIPAL (2) should be used sparingly. Across all five Impact Areas, it is expected that no more than two Impact Areas receive a score of 2 for a single result, except in rare cases.
