CGIAR has set TWO specific objectives and/or collective global targets to address Nutrition, Health, and Food Security for humans in developing countries by 2030 as follows: 

To end hunger for all and enable affordable, healthy diets for the 3 billion people who do not currently have access to safe and nutritious food. 

To reduce cases of foodborne illness (600 million annually) and zoonotic diseases (1 billion annually) by one third in humans
