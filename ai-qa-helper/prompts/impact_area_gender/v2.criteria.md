**CGIAR Key Objectives**:

Gender equality refers to equal rights, responsibilities, and opportunities for all individuals regardless of gender, with particular attention to disparities in access to resources, decision-making, and benefits within food, land, and water systems.

CGIAR has set TWO specific objectives and/or collective global targets to address Gender Equality, Youth and Social Inclusion in developing countries by 2030:

Close the gender gap in rights to economic resources, and access to ownership and control over land and natural resources for over 500 million women working in food, land, and water systems.

Offer rewardable opportunities to 267 million young people who are not in employment, education, or training.

These targets provide contextual guidance only and should not be used in isolation to justify a score.

**Evaluation Criteria**:

Assign the impact area tag to the research result based on the following criteria:

NOT TARGETED (0): The research result has been screened against the CGIAR key objectives for Gender Equality, Youth and Social Inclusion as highlighted in the ** CGIAR Key Objectives **, and it is NOT found to address or directly contribute to any aspect of Gender Equality, Youth and Social Inclusion significantly as highlighted in the ** CGIAR Key Objectives ** i.e., the research is unrelated to the areas of CGIAR key objectives for Gender Equality, Youth and Social Inclusion, focusing instead on different topics with “non-significant” or “indirect” implications for the ** CGIAR Key Objectives **. Some important considerations are as follows:

In the context of gender equality within agriculture, a score of NOT TARGETED (0) should be assigned if the research result does not target gender equality nor do the findings reveal any insights into gender-related implications or impacts and do not provide information for understanding gender dynamics in a social (not physiological) context.

SIGNIFICANT (1): The research result makes a direct and meaningful contribution to one or more aspects of the CGIAR key objectives for Gender Equality, Youth and Social Inclusion as highlighted in the **CGIAR Key Objectives**, however contributing to the **CGIAR Key Objectives** is NOT the principal objective of the research. Gender Equality, Youth and Social Inclusion as highlighted in the **CGIAR Key Objectives** are important and deliberate/explicit objectives of the research but these are NOT the primary focus of the research and only secondary to other principal goals of the research i.e., the research has other prime objectives but has been formulated or adjusted to contribute to one or more aspects of the CGIAR key objectives for Gender Equality, Youth and Social Inclusion as highlighted in the **CGIAR Key Objectives**. Some important considerations are as follows:

In the context of gender equality within agriculture, a score of SIGNIFICANT (1) should be assigned if the research findings reveal insights into gender-related implications or impacts and provide valuable information for understanding gender dynamics in agriculture (e.g., social roles, power dynamics, access to resources, and decision-making) or have potential implications for gender-inclusive policies and practices.

PRINCIPAL (2): The primary/principal objective of the research result is to address and directly contribute to one or more aspects of the CGIAR key objectives for Gender Equality, Youth and Social Inclusion as highlighted in the **CGIAR Key Objectives** i.e., these objectives are fundamental to the design of the research and its expected results. The project is designed to primarily achieve one or more aspects of the CGIAR key objectives for Gender Equality, Youth and Social Inclusion as highlighted in the **CGIAR Key Objectives** and the research would not have been undertaken without the intention to contribute to these **CGIAR Key Objectives**. Some important considerations are as follows:

In the context of gender equality within agriculture, a score of PRINICIPAL (2) should be assigned if the research objectives specifically address gender disparities and aim to contribute to gender equality in the agriculture sector. Moreover, the research must have a clear goal/focus on contributing to understanding gender-related issues in agriculture, such as gender-based access to resources, decision-making, labor division, and women’s empowerment.

Important QA Guidance:

Scores of PRINCIPAL (2) should be used sparingly. Across all five Impact Areas, it is expected that no more than two Impact Areas receive a score of 2 for a single result, except in rare cases.
