You need to determine whether the research results align with and contribute to CGIAR key objectives in poverty reduction, improving livelihoods, and job creation as specified in **CGIAR Key Objectives**. Your goal is to accurately assign ONE of the THREE following impact area tags that reflects the relevance and impact of the research results on these CGIAR key objectives:

PRINCIPAL (2)

SIGNIFICANT (1)

NOT TARGETED (0)

Your assessment must be strictly aligned with CGIAR definitions and should not rely on generic or external poverty or livelihoods interpretations beyond CGIAR’s framework.

**CGIAR Key Objectives**:

CGIAR has set TWO specific objectives and/or collective global targets to address poverty reduction, improving livelihoods, and job creation in developing countries by 2030 as follows:

Lift at least 500 million people living in rural areas above the extreme poverty line of US $1.90 per day (2011 PPP).

Reduce by at least half the proportion of men, women, and children of all ages living in poverty in all its dimensions, according to national definitions.

These targets provide contextual guidance only and should not be used in isolation to justify a score.

Some example topics aligning with and directly contributing to CGIAR key objectives for poverty reduction, improving livelihoods, and job creation are as follows:

Improving social protection and employment opportunities by supporting access to resources and markets

Developing solutions for resilient, income-generating agriculture for small farmers

Reducing poverty through adoption of new varieties and breeds with better yields

These examples are illustrative only and must be interpreted strictly within CGIAR’s definitions.

**Evaluation Criteria**:

Assign the impact area tag to the research result based on the following criteria:

NOT TARGETED (0): The research result has been screened against the CGIAR key objectives for poverty reduction, improving livelihoods, and job creation as highlighted in the ** CGIAR Key Objectives **, and it is NOT found to address or directly contribute to any aspect of poverty reduction, improving livelihoods, and job creation significantly as highlighted in the ** CGIAR Key Objectives ** i.e., the research is unrelated to the areas of CGIAR key objectives of poverty reduction, improving livelihoods, and job creation, focusing instead on different topics with “non-significant” or “indirect” implications for the ** CGIAR Key Objectives **.

An example research study which should be assigned an impact area tag of NOT TARGETED (0) is as follows:

A study focused solely on agricultural productivity without considering its direct impact on poverty, livelihoods, or job creation.

SIGNIFICANT (1): The research result makes a direct and meaningful contribution to one more aspects of the CGIAR key objectives for poverty reduction, improving livelihoods, and job creation as highlighted in the **CGIAR Key Objectives**, however contributing to **CGIAR Key Objectives** is NOT the principal objective of the research. Poverty reduction, improving livelihoods and job creation as highlighted in the **CGIAR Key Objectives** are important and deliberate/explicit objectives of the research but these are NOT the primary focus of the research and only secondary to other principal goals of the research i.e., the research has other prime objectives but has been formulated or adjusted to contribute to one or more aspects of the CGIAR key objectives for poverty reduction, improving livelihoods, and job creation as highlighted in the **CGIAR Key Objectives**

An example research study which should be assigned an impact area tag of SIGNIFICANT (1) is as follows:

A study that investigates the best agricultural practices while exploring its impact on income generation for poverty reduction and improving livelihoods of farmers

PRINCIPAL (2): The primary/principal objective of the research result is to address and directly contribute to one or more aspects of the CGIAR key objectives for poverty reduction, improving livelihoods, and job creation as highlighted in the **CGIAR Key Objectives** i.e., these objectives are fundamental to the design of the research and its expected results. The project is designed to primarily achieve one or more aspects of the CGIAR key objectives for poverty reduction, improving livelihoods, and job creation as highlighted in the **CGIAR Key Objectives** and the research would not have been undertaken without the intention to contribute to these **CGIAR Key Objectives**.

An example research study which should be assigned an impact area tag of PRINCIPAL (2) is as follows:

A study specifically designed to develop strategies for improving rural livelihoods and lifting communities out of extreme poverty.

Important QA Guidance: Scores of PRINCIPAL (2) should be used sparingly. Across all five Impact Areas, it is expected that no more than two Impact Areas receive a score of 2 for a single result, except in rare cases.
